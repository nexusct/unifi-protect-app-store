FAQ

Straight answers

Does this work with my existing UniFi setup?

Yes — that's the point. Any UniFi Protect camera with RTSP enabled works. No new cameras, no NVR replacement, no network changes beyond the GPU appliance sitting next to your console.

Where does the video go?

Nowhere. All inference runs on the GPU appliance in your building. Alerts carry metadata and (where you allow it) a snapshot. There is no cloud video subscription because there is no cloud video.

Is this BIPA-compliant in Illinois?

By design, yes. The platform performs no face recognition and no biometric identification — it analyzes skeletons, objects, plates, and counts. If you ever want biometric features, that requires written consent flows and counsel review, and we'll tell you that plainly.

What if a function doesn't fit my site?

Functions are swappable monthly. Start with the obvious ones, tune zones on the onboarding call, swap what doesn't earn its keep.

Who installs and supports it?

Nexus Communications Technology — Schaumburg, IL. We're a UniFi integrator and managed-services provider; the appliance, onboarding, and ongoing tuning come from the same team that answers the phone.
